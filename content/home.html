    FAQ

    Before You Book

    What’s included in my quote?

    Ask for an itemised quote showing accommodation, transport, activities, taxes and optional extras. Inclusions depend on your selected trip.

    Is a deposit required?

    Ask BDTAT to confirm the deposit, due date and payment instructions in writing before you pay.

    Can I change or cancel my trip?

    Change fees and refunds depend on the suppliers and booking terms. Ask for the terms for your dates before confirming.

    When is my trip confirmed?

    Ask BDTAT for written confirmation and applicable tickets or vouchers before making other travel arrangements.

    What details should I send for a quote?

    Send your departure city, preferred dates, number of adults and children, and any hotel or transport preferences.

    Can I customise a package?

    Tell us which hotel category, dates and activities you prefer. Ask for a revised quote based on availability.
